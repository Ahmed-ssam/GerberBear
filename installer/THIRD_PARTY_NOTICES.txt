Gerber Bear - third-party software included in this installer
==============================================================

Gerber Bear bundles the following software, unmodified. Each remains under its own
licence; the licence texts are installed with each component (see the folders named
below) and are available at the addresses given.

Python 3.12 (embeddable)        PSF License                 python\LICENSE.txt
                                https://www.python.org/
PySide6 / Qt 6                  LGPL-3.0 (Qt for Python)    python\Lib\site-packages\PySide6
                                Source: https://code.qt.io/  https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/
                                You may replace the bundled PySide6/Qt libraries with your own build.
pydantic, keyring               MIT                         python\Lib\site-packages
httpx, numpy                    BSD-3-Clause                python\Lib\site-packages
trimesh                         MIT                         python\Lib\site-packages
Eclipse Temurin JRE 21          GPL-2.0 with Classpath Exception
                                https://adoptium.net/        app\tools\jre\legal
Freerouting 1.9.0               GPL-3.0                     app\tools\freerouting-1.9.0.jar
                                Source: https://github.com/freerouting/freerouting
                                (run as a separate program; not linked into Gerber Bear)
three.js                        MIT                         app\ai_pcb_designer\visualization\threejs\vendor\LICENSE
Inter (font)                    SIL Open Font License 1.1   app\ai_pcb_designer\resources\fonts
Roboto Mono (font)              Apache-2.0                  app\ai_pcb_designer\resources\fonts
Lucide (icons)                  ISC                         app\ai_pcb_designer\resources\icons\LICENSE-lucide.txt

Not included: KiCad (GPL-3.0, https://www.kicad.org) is installed separately and used
as an external program. Board designs you create are yours.

Online services used only when you enable/use them: your AI provider (Google Gemini,
OpenAI or a local server) with your own key; the jlcsearch catalogue
(https://jlcsearch.tscircuit.com) to look up JLCPCB part numbers for the BOM.
